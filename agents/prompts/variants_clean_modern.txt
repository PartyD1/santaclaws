Clean modern:
- Bright, clear, service-forward design.
- White background, dark text, restrained blue, green, and slate accents.
- Use a crisp top contact bar, white service cards, a pale blue-green trust band, and a dark footer/contact panel.
- Layout should feel dense enough for a real business site: no oversized empty hero, no marketing fluff.
- Organized service cards, obvious phone CTA, scan-friendly hours/location band.
- Feels trustworthy for a busy local repair shop owner.
- Should look practical and crisp, not like a generic startup template.
