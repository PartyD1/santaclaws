Premium:
- Polished, high-confidence, dealership-alternative feel.
- Spacious but not sparse layout, strong service positioning, premium dark and light contrast.
- Good for shops with strong ratings or specialty services.
- Emphasize expertise, diagnostics, transparency, and appointment confidence.
- Use a dark hero, bright white content bands, tight metric/trust chips, and precise service cards.
- It should feel like a serious independent shop, not a luxury hotel or generic SaaS product.
- Use precise, grounded language and avoid luxury cliches.
- Make the phone CTA feel concierge-level without inventing facts.
