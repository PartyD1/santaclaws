Retro local:
- Warm, neighborhood repair-shop personality.
- Subtle vintage cues, strong local trust language, no gimmicks.
- Good for long-running shops, family-owned language, and review mentions of honesty.
- Use tasteful red, off-white, charcoal, and muted teal accents without making the page look old or brown.
- Use a strong sign-painter style heading area, compact service tiles, and a practical location/contact block.
- Keep contrast high and avoid overly beige/brown pages.
- CTA should still be modern, readable, and easy to tap.
